Delete your SmartMoney account

This page explains how to delete your SmartMoney account and what happens to your data. SmartMoney is operated by SmartMoney (registered trade name: SMART MONEY), a sole proprietorship owned by Rupankar De.

Delete it in the app

  • Open the SmartMoney app and sign in.
  • Open your Profile.
  • Choose Delete account.
  • Confirm with your password.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot sign in, email admin@smart-money.in from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

Before you delete

Any confirmed but unpaid cashback or wallet balance must be withdrawn before your account can be deleted. If your balance is at or above the ₹250 minimum withdrawal amount, we will help you withdraw it first. If it is below ₹250, we will make a one-time exception and pay it out to you when you close your account; if it is too small to cover your share of the payout fee, it will be cleared on closure.

What we delete and what we keep

  • We delete your profile photo, replace your name, email address and mobile number with anonymised values, and sign you out of every device.
  • We keep a restricted copy of your original name, email address and mobile number for 90 days, visible only to senior administrators, for fraud checks and open disputes, and then delete it.
  • Wallet and transaction records are kept for up to 8 years, linked only to the anonymised account, where needed for tax and accounting records, disputes and fraud prevention.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy (sections 12 and 13) and Terms of Service.
