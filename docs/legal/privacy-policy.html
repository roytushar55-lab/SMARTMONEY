SmartMoney is an affiliate cashback platform. Users browse stores and offers, open affiliate links through SmartMoney, complete purchases on merchant websites, and earn cashback after the merchant validates the transaction. SmartMoney is the business operating the App; if SmartMoney later operates under a separate incorporated legal entity, that entity's registered name, address, and registration number will be added here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Name — the full name you provide when creating your account.
  • Email address — used for account identification, login, verification (OTP), and communication.
  • Mobile number — collected as contact information only. SmartMoney does not currently verify mobile numbers or use them for OTP purposes; account verification is performed via your email address only.
  • Account/login information — your password (stored in encrypted/hashed form, never in plain text) and account status.
  • Profile information — an optional profile photo you upload, stored via our cloud storage provider.
  • Technical information — details that your device and the App send automatically with each request, such as the type of software making the request, used to keep the App working correctly and securely.
  • IP address — collected automatically by our servers as part of standard request logging, for security and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Consent record — when you create an account, we record that you confirmed you are 18 or over and accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, together with the version of the documents, the date and time, and your IP address, as evidence of your consent.
  • App usage data — basic operational and diagnostic information generated by your use of the App, used to operate, secure, and improve the Service. SmartMoney does not currently use a dedicated third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK; if that changes, this Policy will be updated.
  • Any other information you choose to provide, such as content of support requests you send us.

We do not currently collect date of birth, government ID numbers, or biometric data as part of account registration.

3. Cashback & Transaction Data

To operate the cashback service, we collect and generate:

  • Affiliate click data — a record created each time you click an affiliate link within the App, including a tracking reference, redirect token, the destination merchant URL, and the tracked/attributed URL.
  • Merchant/order information — details of the merchant and, where made available to us, order-level information needed to validate a purchase (e.g., order ID, order value, order date).
  • Cashback earned — the cashback amount calculated for a qualifying transaction.
  • Cashback status — Pending, Awaiting Admin Review, Confirmed, Rejected, Paid Out, or Reversed.
  • Transaction/reference IDs — internal and affiliate-network reference identifiers used to track a purchase from click to cashback confirmation.

4. Wallet & Payout Information

Your SmartMoney wallet tracks your available balance, pending balance, total cashback earned, and total amount withdrawn.

When SmartMoney's withdrawal feature is available, processing a withdrawal will require us to collect payout details necessary to send funds to you, which may include:

  • UPI ID
  • Bank account details (account number, IFSC code, account holder name), if you choose bank transfer instead of UPI
  • PAN or other tax information, where required for tax reporting or compliance with applicable withdrawal thresholds
  • Withdrawal/payout history — a record of your withdrawal requests and their status

We only collect the payout details relevant to the payout method you select, and only at the time you request a withdrawal. Once SmartMoney's withdrawal feature is live, these payout details will be processed through Cashfree, our payout provider.

5. Affiliate Tracking

SmartMoney earns commission from merchants when you complete a purchase after clicking through an affiliate link in the App, and we share part of that commission with you as cashback. To make this possible, we use:

  • Affiliate links generated for each store/offer you click on.
  • Affiliate network tracking — provided by our affiliate network partner(s) (currently including Cuelinks), used to route your click to the merchant and to attribute the resulting purchase back to your click.
  • Click/transaction attribution identifiers — a tracking reference and redirect token, unique to each click, used to match a merchant-confirmed purchase to your account.
  • SubIDs or similar tracking identifiers passed to our affiliate network partner(s) and, in turn, to the merchant, so a purchase can be correctly attributed to you for cashback purposes.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

  • Affiliate tracking identifiers — tracking references, redirect tokens, and tracked URLs, used to attribute purchases made after you click through the App.
  • Session tokens — used to keep you signed in and to protect your account.
  • Cookies — if you access any SmartMoney web-based property, that property may use cookies for session management and security. The SmartMoney mobile app itself does not use browser cookies.

11. Data Storage & Security

  • Passwords are stored in hashed (encrypted) form — never in plain text.
  • Token-based authentication (short-lived access tokens and refresh tokens) secures your session.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Account and transaction data is stored in an access-controlled database hosted on Microsoft Azure (Hyderabad, India region); uploaded files are stored via Azure's cloud storage.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to personnel who need it to perform their role.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

12. Data Retention

We retain your account, transaction, and cashback data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. After you close your account or request deletion, we retain certain data for up to 8 years from the date of closure, where necessary to resolve pending cashback or disputes, comply with tax and accounting record-keeping requirements, and prevent fraud. Once these purposes no longer apply, or the 8-year period has elapsed, whichever is sooner, we delete or anonymize the remaining data.

13. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your SmartMoney account in the App by opening your profile, choosing Delete account, and confirming your password. You can also ask us to delete it by contacting admin@smart-money.in.

When you request deletion:

  • Any confirmed but unpaid cashback or wallet balance must be withdrawn before your account can be deleted, as described in our Terms of Service. If your balance is at or above the ₹250 minimum withdrawal amount, we will not process a deletion request while it remains and will help you withdraw it first. If it is below ₹250, we will make a one-time exception and pay it out to you when you close your account; if it is too small to cover your share of the payout fee, it will be cleared on closure.
  • Any pending (unconfirmed) cashback tied to an in-progress merchant transaction may be resolved or forfeited, since it cannot be verified after account closure.
  • When your account is deleted, we delete your profile photo, replace your name, email address and mobile number with anonymised values, and sign you out of every device. We keep a restricted copy of your original name, email address and mobile number for 90 days, visible only to senior administrators, for fraud checks and open disputes, and then delete it. Wallet and transaction records are kept for the period in Section 12, linked only to the anonymised account.
  • We will delete or anonymize your other personal data, except for information we are legally required to retain (e.g., transaction and payout records for tax/accounting purposes, or fraud-investigation records).
