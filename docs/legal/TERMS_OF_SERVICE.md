## 5. How Cashback Works

**Cashback is a share of commission, not a fixed reward.** The percentage of a purchase's commission credited to you as cashback, and the typical window before a pending cashback is expected to be confirmed, are determined by SmartMoney's cashback settings. These settings can vary by affiliate network, by merchant, and by product category, and SmartMoney may change them at any time, including for purchases already in progress, without that being a breach of these Terms.

Each cashback entry moves through the following states, which the App shows you:

- **Pending** — your click and purchase have been recorded, but the merchant or affiliate network has not yet confirmed the transaction.
- **Awaiting Admin Review** — the transaction needs manual review before it can be confirmed.
- **Confirmed** — the cashback is credited to your wallet's available balance.
- **Rejected** — the transaction did not qualify for cashback (for example, it was outside the terms of the offer, or the affiliate network declined it).
- **Paid Out** — the confirmed cashback has been withdrawn from your wallet.
- **Reversed** — cashback that was previously confirmed has been reversed, most commonly because the underlying purchase was cancelled, returned, or refunded by the merchant after confirmation.

**Cashback is never guaranteed**, even when you click through correctly and complete a purchase, because confirmation depends on the merchant and the affiliate network, not on SmartMoney alone. Cashback may be delayed, reduced, rejected, or reversed for reasons including but not limited to:

- The merchant or affiliate network failing to report or confirm the transaction to us.
- Tracking failure caused by browser settings, ad blockers, extensions, cleared cookies, or using another coupon/cashback source in the same purchase session.
- The purchase being cancelled, returned, exchanged, or refunded, in whole or in part.
- The purchase falling outside the specific terms of the offer you clicked (for example, excluded categories or products).
- A violation of these Terms, the merchant's own terms, or the affiliate network's terms.

If a tracking identifier or cookie set by your click is blocked, cleared, or otherwise disabled before you complete your purchase, SmartMoney may be unable to attribute that purchase to you, and you may not receive cashback for it. We encourage you to complete your purchase in the same browsing session you clicked through from, without navigating away to other cashback or coupon sites first.

**If cashback is reversed while it is still sitting in your wallet as an available balance**, we will deduct the reversed amount from that balance. **If the corresponding amount has already been withdrawn**, your wallet balance is not adjusted for it automatically; instead, we will contact you directly to resolve it, which may include a request for repayment. We will always tell you when a reversal is being applied to your account and why.
